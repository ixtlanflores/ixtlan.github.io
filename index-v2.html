Bio

[ Headshot Photo ]

Welcome to my acting portfolio! I am a passionate and dedicated actor with experience in stage, film, and television.

Ixtlán is an actor and multidisciplinary artist whose work bridges classical theatre, underground performance, and embodied ritual. This site brings together credits across stage, film, and television, plus experimental and performance-art work.

Theater / Stage

Recent and featured stage work, from world premieres to Shakespeare in the Park.

  • Manning — Puppet Design / Puppeteer — West Coast Premiere, Crowded Fire Theater (Sep 5–Sep 26, 2026).
  • Manahatta — Se-ket-tu-may-qua / Luke — premiered at three venues: Oregon Shakespeare Festival (world premiere, 2018), Yale Repertory Theatre (East Coast premiere, 2020), and Aurora Theatre Company (Bay Area premiere, 2024).
  • A Midsummer Night's Dream — Demetrius / Flute — Marin Shakespeare Company (2025).
